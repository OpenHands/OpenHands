Factories: delete a factory and its worklogs

Recorded in Agent Canvas (this branch) against the Enterprise stack from docker-compose.yml, with the server from OpenHands/enterprise#1392 at efbbf15 and the automation service from OpenHands/automation#752 at e5da824. The video shows the Delete button next to Rename on a factory with worklogs and 1 live automation, the worklogs-aware confirmation dialog, cancelling the dialog, the 409 Conflict error toast while a live automation remains, moving the automation to No factory via Change factory, and deleting the empty factory so Enterprise purges its worklogs and Canvas returns to the factories list.
