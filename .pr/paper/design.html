Paper: a warm light theme.

A warm light palette brings brown ink, rust accents and restrained serif headings to the operational interface. The theme adds a choice to Settings while preserving Canvas’s layout, workflows and default palette.

★ Compatibility is additive. The new paper key uses the same selector, bootstrap and persistence path as every existing palette. OpenHands-Neutral remains the default.

The font and its license ship together

Paper owns its definition, font stylesheet, WOFF2, license, and provenance together under src/themes/color-theme/definitions/paper/. The unmodified Newsreader roman variable WOFF2 retains its weight and optical-size axes. It is sourced from Production Type and licensed under SIL OFL 1.1. The standalone app serves it locally on demand; the library includes the font data in its stylesheet. No additional font CDN is introduced.
