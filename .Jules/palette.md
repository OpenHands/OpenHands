## 2025-02-23 - [Served Tab Accessibility]
**Learning:** Found that the Served Tab (iframe view) had icon-only buttons without ARIA labels, making them inaccessible. Added standardized translation keys for common actions like "Open in New Tab" and "Home" which can be reused.
**Action:** Always check icon-only buttons for accessibility properties, especially in less frequented parts of the UI like the Served Tab.
